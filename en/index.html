About us

Plaizance was born from a simple belief: enjoying your boat should always remain a pleasure, never a chore.

Behind this promise lies the expertise of Nauticoncept, a recognised player in connected boating solutions for over 10 years. As specialists in smart boat monitoring, we designed Plaizance as a natural extension of our know-how: a connected concierge service combining technology, personal support and operational excellence.

Our mission is clear: to free you from boat management so you can focus on what really matters — sailing and enjoying your time on the water.
